## Item tracking method

Record an item's area, landmark, purpose, and whether every player needs to interact with it. This keeps the [map guide](/map/big-walk-map) useful for both solo notes and co-op callouts.

## Items and puzzles

Some discoveries make more sense when paired with a clue. Save the clue context before moving an object, and use the [puzzle guide](/puzzles) when the group is ready for a solution.

## Cleanup route

Return to unmarked areas after the main route, then compare your notes with the [walkthrough](/walkthrough/big-walk-walkthrough) and [achievement checklist](/achievements/big-walk-achievements).

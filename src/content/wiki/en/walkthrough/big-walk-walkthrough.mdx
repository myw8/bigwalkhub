## How to use this walkthrough

Keep the [Big Walk map](/map/big-walk-map) open while following the route. The page is designed for spoiler-aware progress: each checkpoint tells you what to confirm before moving on, while optional discoveries can be left for cleanup.

## Opening route and first objectives

Learn the movement and communication loop before splitting up. Mark the first obvious landmark on your map, test the interaction prompt, and make sure every player can rejoin the host. If the group is on different platforms, confirm [crossplay compatibility](/crossplay/is-big-walk-cross-platform) first.

## Puzzle checkpoints

When a route stops at a puzzle, record the clue, identify the interactable objects, and agree on a solution order before activating anything. Use the [puzzle solutions](/puzzles) category for focused answers without losing your place in the main route.

## Mid-game exploration

Use the map to separate required objectives from optional paths. Collect items that unlock traversal or future clues, and note any area that appears to have a one-way transition. The [items database](/items) can help with later cleanup.

## Final route and ending preparation

Before the final sequence, finish any group-only tasks and create a manual save if the platform supports it. Review the [ending explanations](/endings) only when you are ready for spoilers, then use the [achievement checklist](/achievements/big-walk-achievements) to plan cleanup.

## Completion cleanup

After the ending, revisit missed map locations, optional puzzles, trophies, and platform-specific tasks. A second co-op run is often more efficient when one player calls out map markers while the other checks the achievement list.

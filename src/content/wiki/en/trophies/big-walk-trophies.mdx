## Trophy route

Complete the main route naturally, keep notes for optional areas, and avoid spending a full replay on a miss until the official trophy description confirms that it is missable. The [PS5 guide](/platforms/ps5) covers console setup.

## Cleanup order

1. Finish the main story route.
2. Revisit map areas with missing markers.
3. Complete optional puzzles and exploration tasks.
4. Arrange a co-op session for group objectives.

For the broader checklist, use [Big Walk achievements](/achievements/big-walk-achievements).

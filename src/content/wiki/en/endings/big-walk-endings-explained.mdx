## Spoiler warning

This page is intended for players who have reached the final route or are comfortable with story spoilers. For a first playthrough, return to the [complete walkthrough](/walkthrough/big-walk-walkthrough) instead.

## Before the final sequence

Finish any group-only objectives, mark optional branches on the [map](/map/big-walk-map), and make sure every player is on the same version. A planned final session is easier to recover than an interrupted one.

## After the ending

Use the [achievement checklist](/achievements/big-walk-achievements) and [trophy guide](/trophies) to plan cleanup. Revisit map markers, puzzle notes, and co-op objectives in separate sessions so the group always knows the goal.
